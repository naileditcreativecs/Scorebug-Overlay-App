# Logo placement editor

Open the in-game controls and choose **Teams, ranks & logos**.

- **Capture scorebug view** records the live area around the logo. The yellow
  dashed marker shows the position at capture time, while the bright logo moves
  and scales over that reference.
- The direct editor over the scorebug now displays the selected logo itself.
  Drag it to move, use a corner to resize, or use the round handle to rotate.
- Every slider has a numeric value box. Click a value box to use the on-screen
  keypad without taking keyboard focus away from the game.
- The logo picker shows and selects the actual current logo. It no longer adds a
  duplicate "Auto" logo entry.

Logo placement remains saved separately for the current HTML scorebug, team,
logo variant, and home/away side.
